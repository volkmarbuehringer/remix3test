# Lookup: Airbnb Typography Scale

**Core Idea**: Airbnb Cereal VF variable font—warm and approachable with rounded terminals. Weight range 500-700 creates confident voice. Negative letter-spacing on headings for intimacy.

**Font Family**:
- Primary: `Airbnb Cereal VF`
- Fallbacks: `Circular, -apple-system, system-ui, Roboto, Helvetica Neue`
- OpenType: `salt` feature on badges/captions for stylistic alternates

**Type Scale**:

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Letter Spacing |
|------|------|--------|-------------|----------------|
| Section Heading | 28px (1.75rem) | 700 | 1.43 | normal |
| Card Heading | 22px (1.38rem) | 600 | 1.18 | -0.44px |
| Card Heading Medium | 22px (1.38rem) | 500 | 1.18 | -0.44px |

| Badge | 11px (0.69rem) | 600 | 1.18 | normal |
| Micro Uppercase | 8px (0.50rem) | 700 | 1.25 | 0.32px |

**Design Principles**:
- **Warm weight range**: 500–700 dominate. No weight 300 or 400 for headings
- **Negative tracking**: -0.18px to -0.44px on headings for intimacy
- **"salt" feature**: Stylistic alternates on badges/captions
- **Variable font**: Cereal VF enables continuous interpolation, but design uses discrete stops at 500, 600, 700

**Quick Reference**:
- Headings: 600-700 weight, negative letter-spacing
- Body: 400 weight, normal spacing
- UI/Buttons: 500 weight, normal spacing
